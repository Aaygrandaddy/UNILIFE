3. Device storage

The game stores small amounts of data in your browser: your settings, your sign-in session (managed by Firebase), and, in offline mode, your account and chats. This storage is strictly necessary for the game to work. Clearing your browser data removes it.

4. Who we share data with

Other players see your public profile, your posts and your online status, and the people you message see your messages. Our service provider Google LLC (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Hosting) processes data on our behalf under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. We may disclose data where Nigerian law requires it, for example to comply with a lawful order.

6. Children

Force Life accounts are for people aged 18 and over. Sign-up checks your age and refuses anyone younger. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it. Parents or guardians can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

  • Access your data and take a copy to use elsewhere: Phone → Profile → Download my data.
  • Correct inaccurate data: email us.
  • Erase your data: Phone → Profile → Delete my account deletes your profile, username, posts and saved progress immediately.
  • Withdraw consent and object to processing based on legitimate interest: email us. Withdrawing does not affect processing that already took place.
  • Restrict processing while a complaint is looked into.
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

We reply to requests within the time allowed by law (generally 30 days) and do not charge for them.

8. Security and breaches

Data travels over encrypted connections. Database security rules stop players from reading other people's private chats, snaps or saved progress, and from changing anyone else's data. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell you without undue delay if the risk is high, as the NDPA requires.

When we change this policy we update the version and date above. For important changes we will ask you to accept the new version in the game.
