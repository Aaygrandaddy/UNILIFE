Force Life ("the game", "we") is an online game set on a fictional campus. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it)To create and secure your account
Username, display name, department, level and skin tone you chooseTo show your profile to other players
Messages, posts, photos and snaps you create in the gameTo deliver them to the people you send them to or show them in feeds
Game progress (money, tokens, missions, position) and the time you were last onlineTo save your progress and show friends when you are online
Reports you file and accounts you blockTo keep players safe

Photos and snaps are screenshots of the game world, not your device's camera. We do not collect your location, contacts or device camera.

Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract) and, for safety features such as reports, for our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe. Where the law requires consent, we ask for it.

Children

Under the NDPA, a child is anyone under 18. If you are under 18 you may only create an account with the permission of a parent or guardian. If we learn that a child has an account without that permission, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Who we share data with

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database and Hosting) to run the game. Google processes data on our behalf and may store it outside Nigeria, under safeguards described in Google's terms. We do not sell your data. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (Phone → Profile → Delete my account), we delete your profile, username, posts and saved progress straight away. Messages you already sent remain with the people who received them. Reports are kept for up to 12 months for safety purposes.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to processing, and ask for a copy. Most of this you can do in the game; for anything else email [CONTACT EMAIL]. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and database rules stop players from reading other people's private chats, snaps or saves.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
